# Writing a campaign
Keywords: composer, subject, preheader, template, mjml, liquid, personalization
The composer edits a campaign's subject, preheader and content. Content is MJML, kept with its compiled HTML. Liquid tags such as {{ subscriber.first_name }} are filled in for each recipient when the email is sent; a fallback covers contacts without the field. AI suggestions (subject ideas, rewrite) propose text; nothing changes until you accept it.
