# Sending providers and routing
Keywords: provider, ses, smtp, routing, quota, webhook, bounce
ReputeCast sends through the providers you connect in Providers: Amazon SES, an SMTP server or another adapter. Each provider's page shows its quota, its webhook status and recent events. Routing rules (Providers › Routing) choose which provider sends which mail, and a provider on hold stops taking new messages until someone resumes it. Bounces and complaints come back through the provider's webhook and suppress the address.
