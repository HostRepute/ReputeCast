# The assistant and privacy
Keywords: assistant, thread, privacy, delete, attach, retention
A thread is yours: nobody else reads it, managers included; they see usage and cost only. Content is kept 30 days after the last activity and can be deleted at once. The assistant reads only what you attach and confirm sharing, and it never sends, schedules, publishes, approves or changes settings: it proposes, and you decide in the screen that owns the action.
