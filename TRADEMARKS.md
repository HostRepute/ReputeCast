# Trademark policy

ReputeCast and the ReputeCast logos and mark (the files in [`brand/`](brand/))
are trademarks of HostRepute. The software licenses in this repository
([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)) cover copyright only and grant no rights to the
name or logos. This policy says how you may use them.

It's meant to be generous. Use the name to tell people the truth about
ReputeCast and what you do with it. Don't use it in a way that makes people
think you are us, that your product is ours, or that we endorse you.

## What you may do without asking

- **Run ReputeCast** for yourself, your organisation or your clients, and say
  so: "Our newsletter is sent with ReputeCast", "We run ReputeCast for our
  clients".
- **Redistribute unmodified ReputeCast** (the official binaries, the image,
  the packages) under its name and with its logos.
- **Offer hosting, setup or support** and describe it with phrases such as
  "Hosting for ReputeCast", "Managed hosting for ReputeCast" or "ReputeCast
  setup and support by Example Ltd". Your own brand must come first and be
  more prominent than ours. If you run a modified version, say so, for
  example "based on ReputeCast".
- **Say that your product works with ReputeCast:** an adapter, an integration,
  a template or an app may say it "works with ReputeCast" or is "compatible
  with ReputeCast" when it is.
- **Write and talk about ReputeCast** in articles, tutorials, books, videos,
  courses and talks, and organise community meetups, showing the logo to
  refer to the project.
- **Use the name for your own instance's address**, such as
  `reputecast.example.com` or `example.com/reputecast` for your
  organisation's own use.

## What needs our written permission

- **Using ReputeCast, or a name that looks or sounds like it, as or in the
  name of your product, service, company or domain.** For example
  "ReputeCast Cloud", "Hosted ReputeCast", "Managed ReputeCast", "ReputeCast
  Hosting", "ReputeCast Pro" or `reputecast-hosting.com`. Don't start the name
  of your offering with "ReputeCast".
- **Using the logos for a commercial service**, in advertising, or on
  merchandise you sell.
- **Implying that something is official**, or that HostRepute made, endorses,
  certifies or supports it.
- **Distributing a modified version under the name or with the logos.** A fork
  needs its own name and logo. You may say it is "based on ReputeCast" or "a
  fork of ReputeCast".

## Attribution is never required

ReputeCast never requires a "Powered by ReputeCast" link or badge. The "Sent
with ReputeCast" line in the footer of public pages is optional: turn it off
in Forms & pages › Branding & domain.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, or you want permission, email
support@hostrepute.com. Tell us there too if you see the name used in a way
that misleads people.

This policy follows the approach of the
[Model Trademark Guidelines](http://modeltrademarkguidelines.org/). We may
update it; the version in this repository is the current one.
