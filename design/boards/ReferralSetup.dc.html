When you turn it on
31,988 links are created, one per confirmed subscriber; people who confirm later get theirs as they join. Referral blocks start showing in your emails, and the invite page and hub go live.

Steps 1 to 4, and the guide's states

Each panel is the guide's main area at that step; the step list and the header stay as on step 5. Progress is saved, so a reload resumes the same step.
