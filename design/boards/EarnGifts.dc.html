Redeeming and reminders

The same rules on the gift page, in the emails and here.
Redeem link
Works once, for 12 months from delivery. Until it's used, anyone who has it can redeem it.
No card
The recipient confirms their address and joins Weekly Digest, with the redeem page's text as consent evidence.
Access
Starts at once and ends after the gift's period. “Gift ending” goes 7 days before, with Keep Insider.
Already paid
A recipient who already has Insider is told so, and can pass the gift on.
Refunds
Made in Stripe. The redeem link stops working and a scheduled email isn't sent.
