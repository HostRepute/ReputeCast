Add a site

Browsers accept a service worker only from the site's own origin, so each site hosts one file.
1Add the snippet to every pageIt loads the prompt and asks for permission.
<script async src="https://reputecast.acme.io/push/v1/prompt.js"
  data-workspace="acme-newsletter"
  data-site="help.acme.io"></script>
2Upload the service workerHost it at the site's root. Check then reports whether it was found, its version and its scope.
rc-push-sw.js3 KB · version 1 · must answer at https://help.acme.io/rc-push-sw.js
3Already have a service worker?Add one line to it instead of uploading the file.
// first line of your service worker
importScripts("https://reputecast.acme.io/push/v1/sw.js");
Subscriptions from sites that aren't listed here are refused.
