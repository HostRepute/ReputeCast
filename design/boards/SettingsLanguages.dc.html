How contacts get a language

Signup formsFrom the browser’s first language, when the contact has none. A form never overwrites it.
ImportsA mapped column. “Spanish”, “es_MX” and “ES” become es and es-MX.
The API and integrationsThe language field, normalized the same way.
The preference centerEach contact’s own “Email language” choice.
Your teamOn the contact’s profile, with where it came from.
