The score and engagement tiers do different jobs
Engagement tiers protect your sender reputation: they decide who gets Broadcasts and who enters the sunset policy. The score measures interest: use it to find your most interested readers and customers. A score never stops or starts mail by itself. Tiers on List health
Score contacts by their interestA score from 0 to 100 adds points for what interested people do, like clicking your pricing page or starting a trial, and takes some away for soft bounces and unsubscribes. Until there is a rule, contacts have no score: they show “—”, and segments and triggers that use the score match nobody.
