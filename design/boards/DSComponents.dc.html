Design system · 2 of 5

Components

Every control the screens use, with its sizes and states. Nothing here is decorative: a badge is a state word, a banner always has an action, a card has one heading and one action group.

← FoundationsNext: patterns →

Buttons

36px tall by default, 30px small, 40px for the one primary wizard action, 44px on the landing page and for icon-only targets. One primary button per screen.

Variants

With icon
Primary = accent fill. Destructive = bad-strong, only for actions that lose data and always behind a confirm.

Sizes

Icon-only buttons are 44px squares with an aria-label; used in the tablet rail and the phone tab bar.

States

Hover darkens the fill. Focus is a 3px accent ring at 32% and is never removed. Nothing moves in the app; only landing buttons lift 1px.

Badges

22px pills with a dot or an icon and a state word. Never for counts or labels that are not states.
