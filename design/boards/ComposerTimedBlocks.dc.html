Colours that leave the digits under 4.5:1 on the background are refused, with the ratio and suggestions the colour picker shows elsewhere; the labels' ratio is shown beside it. The composer warns once the end has passed; Review & send blocks a send whose countdown ends before the send time and warns when it ends within an hour of the send's start. On a phone the composer is a quick review: the Checks sheet carries the warning, and the block's settings open on a tablet or a computer.
