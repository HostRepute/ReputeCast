Tue 14 Oct, 16:02 · Claude Sonnet 5.5

October Product Update was delivered to 47,548 of 47,980 addresses (99.1%). 19,590 people opened it: 41.2% of delivered, and 52.8% of the 37,112 whose opens can be measured. Apple Mail opened another 10,436 automatically, so we can't tell whether those were read, and 1,240 scanner opens are left out. Human clicks came from 2,996 people (6.3%), and the feature-flags link drew 38% of those clicks. Subject B, “What shipped in October, and what we're retiring”, won on clicks with 98% confidence (6.7% against 5.6%). Outlook bounced 1.0%, twice the rate of the other mailbox providers. 112 people unsubscribed, most often for “too many emails”.

What “of measurable” means
Of 47,548 delivered
Human opens 19,590 · 41.2%Measurable, no human open 17,522 · 36.9%Privacy-protected 10,436 · 21.9%

Measurable means delivered minus privacy-protected: 47,548 − 10,436 = 37,112 people whose opens we can see. 19,590 of them opened it, which is 52.8% of measurable. 41.2% of delivered divides the same opens by everyone, including the 10,436 whose reading we can't see, so it's a floor.

Scanner opens come from security filters that fetch a message seconds after delivery; the 1,240 here count nowhere. Clicks count for every reader, because Apple Mail doesn't click links. Arrows on opens compare the measurable rate, so a change in how many of your readers use Apple Mail doesn't move them.
