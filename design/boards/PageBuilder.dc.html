What exactly do I get?

A PDF of 86 pages, 4.2 MB: eight chapters, each with a one-page checklist. It reads well on a laptop, a tablet or on paper.

How do I get the file?

Right after you pay, a page opens with the download, and we email you a link. The link works for 7 days and 5 downloads.

I lost the link. Can I get a new one?

Yes. Sign in at news.acme.io/account and choose “Send me a new link”. It always goes to the address you bought with.

Who takes my payment?

Acme Media, through Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, never to Acme or to this site. Stripe emails your receipt, with any tax.

Do I have to subscribe to anything?

No. Buying doesn't add you to any newsletter. Tick “Also send me the Weekly Digest” only if you want it.
