Text expansion rules

Settled by the stress tests: Campaigns, Review & send and Team in Vietnamese and in the pseudo-locale (+40%). They belong with the layout rules (DSLayout); they’re listed here with the rest of phase 2.

The seven rules

1 · Never cut off verdicts, status badges, numbers, buttons, permission keys, error messages, or anything in a dialog. They wrap, or the layout makes room. A select can’t wrap: a choice whose label can pass about 32 characters is a radio card instead, and filter selects size to their longest option.
2 · Ellipsis, with the full text on hover and focus, for user content in table cells (campaign, list and member names, email addresses) and the middle of breadcrumbs.
3 · At most two lines for table headers, stat-tile labels, sidebar labels and card captions. A column is as wide as its header needs on two lines, so numbers aren’t squeezed.
4 · Buttons don’t wrap. A group that doesn’t fit moves to its own row, as the composer header’s does. A full-width button longer than its container is the exception: it wraps to two centered lines.
5 · Badges don’t wrap; the cell wraps around them.
6 · Tabs scroll sideways, as today.
7 · Body text keeps a line height of at least 1.4 in Vietnamese, so stacked marks (ệ, ở, ữ) aren’t clipped. Overlines stay uppercase and keep their marks.
