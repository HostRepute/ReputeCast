How placement tests work

One copy of the email goes to each seed mailbox you own, through the route or the providers you pick. Nothing reaches your contacts.
Copies are real sends with the same headers and link tracking. Their opens and clicks are never counted, and they never show in reports or contact timelines.
Results fill in live. A copy that's missing after 15 minutes is Not arrived; one that turns up within the hour updates the result.
A test never changes a send. Copies through a warming-up provider count toward its daily cap.
Copies are deleted from the seeds 24 hours after each test.
10 seeds are a sample, not your audience. Use tests to compare emails and providers.Deletion settings
