Design system · brand

ReputeCast logo

An envelope whose flap is a check: mail that was checked before it went out, and landed. One shape, one colour, drawn on a 32-unit grid so it survives at 16 px in a browser tab and at 1024 px on an app store.

FoundationsAccessibility

The mark

Construction on the 32-unit grid. Red points are the anchors: 7,12 · 16,19 · 27,8 for the check; the body closes at 17.5,12 and 25,16.5 so the check leaves through the open corner.

Geometry

Grid 32 × 32 units, 1 unit = 1 px at 32 pxSquare corner radius 8 (25 percent), teal #0F766E Strokes 2.4 units, round caps and joins, whiteCheck short stroke 7,12 → 16,19; long stroke 16,19 → 27,8 at 45° Envelope top edge from 7 to 17.5, body 7 to 25 wide, 12 to 24 tallOpening the top-right corner stays open from 17.5,12 to 25,16.5
128 · 64 · 32 · 16 px. Below 24 px use the favicon file, which has 3-unit strokes.
Why this and not a shield or a graph: ReputeCast's promise is simple, your email was checked and it got there. The envelope is the email; the check is Send Readiness. The open corner is the mail leaving; nothing is locked or guarded by force.

Lockups

The horizontal lockup is the default. The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans Bold with -2 percent tracking, cap height aligned to the square's middle band. The stacked version is for avatars with text, splash screens and cards. Nothing else sits inside the clear space.

Clear space and minimum size

½ x
Clear space all round is half the mark's height (x). Minimum sizes: mark 16 px, horizontal lockup 120 px wide, stacked lockup 80 px wide.
