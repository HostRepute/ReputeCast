Design system · dark theme · M54

Dark theme

The same tokens, a second set of values. A screen asks for the token that does the job (surface for a card, raised for a menu, field for an input) and the theme picks the value. Everything on this board is drawn with the dark values from tokens.json, as the app renders them. Previews of what readers see stay light; code stays dark in both themes.

← ComponentsOverview in dark →

Palette

Every colour token has a light and a dark value (tokens.json, themes: light, dark). Each swatch shows the light value on the left and the dark one on the right; text, line and chart tokens give their contrast in both. Every text token is at least 4.5:1 on every surface in the dark, control borders, the focus ring and bars at least 3:1.

Steps, darkest first

ground #0C1119
The page behind everything and the darkest step. Banners and the content column sit on it.
field #0F151E
Inputs, selects, text areas and option cards: a step below their card, so a place to type reads as a well.
side #111822
The sidebar and the icon rail, half a step above the ground.
surface #151C27
Cards, the top bar, buttons, table rows and the phone tab bar: one step up.
raised #1A2230
Things that float: menus, popovers, dialogs, the jobs tray and sheets, one more step up and with a deeper shadow.
hover #232D3B
Hover and keyboard highlight come forward: lighter than a card or a menu, so a highlighted item lifts rather than sinks.

Buttons and badges

Strong fills flip: accent and bad-strong are light in the dark, so their text is on-strong (#0C1119). Badges keep a dot or icon and a state word.

Variants

Primary: accent #2EC4AF with on-strong text, 8.7:1. Destructive: bad-strong #F87171 with on-strong, 6.8:1, or the outline in bad-fg on bad-line. Disabled keeps its colours at 55% and says why.

Hover

Primary hover is accent-hover #5CD8C6 (lighter, not darker); secondary hover is hover #232D3B; ghost hover is line-2 #1F2834; destructive outline hover is bad-wash #2A1214.
