1 · Delete and archive Drafts and campaigns that sent nothing can be deleted, restorable for 30 days; sent ones are archived, never deleted: their reports, web posts, retries and approvals keep working (D4).
2 · Many campaigns Search names and subjects, filter by date, sender, audience and tag, select rows and act on them together; a scheduled row can be unscheduled where it is.
3 · Follow-ups from a report Resend to people who didn't open (human opens only, privacy-protected opens counted as unknown, not as opened) or send to clickers: a new draft with that audience and a subject of its own.
4 · Sending time and cadence Send at the same local time in each recipient's time zone, as texts already do. A campaign is a regular issue or an announcement; readers who chose a weekly or monthly rhythm get only digests of that rhythm and announcements (D1).
5 · Saved templates and sections The gallery searches saved templates and sections by name; each tile's menu renames it, replaces it with the email open in the composer, or deletes it.
6 · Discount codes Campaigns › Discount codes holds the pools that give each reader a code of their own (M59). Everyone who can see campaigns opens it; creating and changing pools needs coupons:manage.
