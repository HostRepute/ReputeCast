Stress test notes · Campaigns in the pseudo-locale (+40%)

  • Cut-off text: none: every string ends in ⟧. Drawn as is, the table needed 1,245 px in a 1,149 px card and cut off the row actions. The two fixes below make it fit.
  • Table headers: at most two lines, and each column is as wide as its header needs on two lines, so “Recipients” and “Delivered” don’t squeeze their numbers (rule 3).
  • Dense tables (proposed): cell padding 10 px instead of 12, and a name column of 160 to 220 px whose names end in an ellipsis (rule 2). Text cells wrap around their badges and buttons (rule 5).
  • Buttons: row actions stack in their cell; the header’s search and “New campaign” move to their own row instead of wrapping (rule 4).
  • Badges: “⟦Ðŕáƒţ · bļöçķéð ·····⟧” stays on one line and sets the Status column’s width (rule 5).
  • Tabs and sidebar: the filters fit beside the title at 1440 px and scroll sideways on phones (rule 6). Sidebar labels fit on one line.
  • Phone: the table scrolls sideways inside its card, as today. The logo showed up in the tab bar because the shell hid it by its English aria-label; keying the rule on the link fixed it. Don’t select elements by their text.
A design note, not part of the screen. Rules are numbered as in section 7, Text expansion rules.
