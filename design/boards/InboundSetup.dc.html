In Amazon SES (us-east-1)
  1. Create a receipt rule for reply.news.acme.io with an SNS action (encoding Base64) that publishes to a new SNS topic. With this action SES bounces messages over 150 KB.
  2. Subscribe this address to the topic over HTTPS. ReputeCast confirms the subscription for that topic only.
  3. Enter the topic ARN
