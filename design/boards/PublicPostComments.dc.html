What shipped in October, and what we're retiring

Feature flags are generally available, audit log exports stream to S3 or your SIEM, and the v1 API retires on 30 November. Plus the Q4 roadmap.

AThe Acme team
Friday 9 October 2026 · 4 min read

October was a big month. Feature flags left beta, audit log exports can stream to S3 or your SIEM, and the v1 API has a retirement date.

Feature flags are generally available

Every workspace can create flags, roll them out to a share of accounts and switch them off in seconds. Flags from the beta keep their history and their rollouts.

Audit log exports

Stream every audit event to an S3 bucket, or to Splunk, Datadog or any SIEM that takes HTTPS. Exports run all the time and pick up where they left off after an outage.

The v1 API retires on 30 November

v1 has been deprecated since March. From 30 November its endpoints answer 410 Gone. The migration guide maps every v1 endpoint to its v2 version.
