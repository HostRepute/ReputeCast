1 · Large audiences Contacts and Suppressions count exactly up to 10,000 and read "10,000+" above, with an exact count on request (review D5). Pages move with cursors, so page 400 opens as fast as page 1.
2 · Select all matching After a whole page is selected, a banner offers every contact the filter matches; bulk actions, export and verification then reach contacts on later pages. A filtered list can be exported as it is.
3 · Saved views Each view chip has a menu: rename it, save the current filters and columns in it, delete it. Personal views are their creator's only.
4 · Columns and order Sort by last activity, score, joined, last open or email from the column headers; choose columns, custom fields included. A view keeps both.
5 · Filters Joined between two dates (the workspace's time zone); the contacts of one import, reached from its report.
6 · Contact profile An address can be corrected; lists and tags can be taken off by the team.
7 · Suppressions, List health, verification An upload group can be removed with a reason; the sunset policy can be turned off and its numbers changed, with a dry run before any erasing; addresses can be checked from Contacts, a segment or a flagged source.
