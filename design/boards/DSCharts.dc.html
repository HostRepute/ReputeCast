Design system · 4 of 5

Charts

Thin marks, one axis, fixed palettes, text in ink. Series colours are assigned by entity in a fixed order and never cycled; status colours are never used for a series.

Validated with the six palette checks (lightness band, chroma floor, adjacent-pair colour-vision-deficiency separation, normal-vision floor, contrast) on the white chart surface.

Engagement tiers · ordinal, one hue

Dark means engaged. The same ramp works for any ordered scale (warm-up days, quota used).

Rules

1One axis. Two measures of different scale get two charts or an index, never a second y-axis.
2Colour follows the entity, never its rank. Gmail is blue in every chart in the product. A filter that changes the series count must not repaint the survivors.
3Sequential is one hue light to dark; diverging is two hues with a grey midpoint. Never a rainbow.
4Status colours are reserved. ok, warn and bad mark thresholds and verdicts, drawn as a thin line or a badge, never as a series.
5Text wears text tokens. Values, labels and legends are ink, ink-2 or ink-3; a coloured square beside them carries identity.
6Thin marks, selective labels. 2px lines, 8px markers, 10 to 14px bars; label the ends and the outliers, not every point. A legend from two series up; up to four series are also direct-labelled.
7A table view exists for every chart, and a hover tooltip for every mark. A bare stat tile is the only form without one.
8Render it and look at it. The validator checks colour, not label collisions. Every chart on these pages was screenshotted at 1440, 834 and 390.
