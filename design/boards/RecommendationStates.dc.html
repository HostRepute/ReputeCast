End the partnership with Ops Weekly?

Ops Weekly has recommended the Weekly Digest since 22 Sep 2026. Ending the partnership:
  • Subscribe requests from Ops Weekly stop at once. The 102 readers who confirmed stay on the Weekly Digest.
  • Ops Weekly leaves your Recommending you list, and Acme Weekly Digest leaves theirs. Both sides are told over the signed channel.
  • The numbers stop growing. What they show today stays in your results.
Ending can't be undone. To work together again, you'd create a new partner code for Ops Weekly.
