1 · Scheduled messages A message sent with send_at waits in the outbox. Until it goes, it can be cancelled or moved, by API and here; both are logged, and cancelling sends message.cancelled to webhooks.
2 · A suppressed recipient The message detail removes the address from the suppression list where the rejection is explained, with a reason that goes to the audit log.
3 · Send test from a template draft The template editor's Send test sends the draft, filled with one of its test data sets, through the System route: "[Test]" in the subject, no stats. Without transactional:send, only to one's own address.
4 · Approvals and results The approvals inbox searches by campaign name. Survey answers, poll comments and responses say when they failed to load, instead of "No comments".
5 · Webhooks and the API A rotated secret keeps signing beside the new one for 24 hours. An endpoint that keeps failing tells the people who manage webhooks. The API reference is this instance's own. An endpoint can take its email events in batches.
