Stress test notes · Review & send in Vietnamese

  • Cut-off text: one, fixed. “Chỉ tạm dừng tuyến hoặc nhà cung cấp hộp thư bị ảnh hưởng” didn’t fit its select, and a select can’t wrap. The two choices are radio cards here. Proposed: a choice whose label can pass about 32 characters is a radio card, not a select (rule 1).
  • Buttons: in check rows, “Yêu cầu gỡ khỏi danh sách” and “Chỉ gửi qua SES” move to their own row instead of squeezing the text beside them (rule 4).
  • Verdict: “Lên lịch gửi cho 31.604 người nhận” fits beside “Sửa cả hai cảnh báo”; the counts wrap under the title.
  • Labels into numbers: none. Long summary labels such as “Danh sách chặn gửi (dùng chung)” keep their number on the right.
  • Diacritics: “Người nhận cuối cùng” and “Giới hạn tốc độ theo nhà cung cấp hộp thư” keep their marks at 15 px and 13 px (rule 7).
  • Formats: 2,84 US$ · 0,10 US$ mỗi 1.000 · 14/giây · Thứ Ba, 14 tháng 10, 2026. DNS records, headers and domains aren’t translated.
A design note, not part of the screen. Rules are numbered as in section 7, Text expansion rules.
