Connect your AWS account

ReputeCast needs permission to send through SES, create the event topic or queue, and manage configuration sets and this workspace's SES tenant. Nothing else. Most setups take 10–15 minutes; Amazon SES takes longer when ReputeCast doesn't run on AWS, and a new AWS account can wait up to 24 hours for production access.
