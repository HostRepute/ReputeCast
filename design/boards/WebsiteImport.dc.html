Step 1 · upload

Upload the export

  1. 1Upload›
  2. 2Review›
  3. 3Import
Drop the export here, or choose a fileSubstack export (.zip), Ghost export (.json or .zip) or WordPress export (.xml), up to 100 MB. ReputeCast reads what kind it is from the file itself.
Where to find the export
SubstackSettings › Exports › Create new export. Download the .zip when it's ready.
GhostSettings › Advanced › Import/Export › Export. The .zip or the .json inside it.
WordPressTools › Export › All content › Download Export File (.xml).
Only the posts are read: subscribers stay where they are. To move them, use Import contacts or, for paid members, Earn.
