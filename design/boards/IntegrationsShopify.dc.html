Customers and consent

Only customers who accepted marketing join a list
They agreed to marketing from Acme Merch. Add them only to lists that consent covers.
Tell Shopify when someone unsubscribes hereUses the optional write access to customers, which was granted
The consent record reads “Shopify: accepted marketing at checkout”, with its date and opt-in level. Customers who didn't accept marketing never join a list: an existing contact keeps the consent they already have, and anyone else counts in the store's totals without their email.

Stored

  • Email, name, country
  • Consent, its date and opt-in level
  • Orders, items and totals
  • Landing page and UTM

Never stored

  • Addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Payment details
  • Order notes
