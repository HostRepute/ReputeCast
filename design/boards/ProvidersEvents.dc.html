Provider-side tracking check

ReputeCast tracks opens and clicks itself. A provider doing it too double-counts and rewrites links twice.
OK
Amazon SESConfiguration set rc-broadcasts: open and click tracking off. Custom redirect domain not needed.
Warn
Postmark adds its own open pixel on the Transactional stream Server setting TrackOpens is on, and TrackLinks is HtmlAndText. Opens on System and Automations mail would count twice, and links get rewritten to click.pstmrk.it before ReputeCast's redirect.
OK
PostalTrack clicks and opens disabled on the mail server; no link rewriting.
