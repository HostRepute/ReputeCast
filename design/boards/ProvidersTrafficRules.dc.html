The canary starts at the scheduled time and everyone else waits for it, so a send with a canary finishes about 25 minutes later. When the watch ends and every gate with enough evidence passes, the send continues by itself; if a gate fails, it stays held and you're asked. Gates still collecting evidence don't hold the send; they become its thresholds.

How these rules apply

  1. A campaign's limits from Review & send replace these defaults for that campaign.
  2. Reply rules act as soon as their count is reached, before any rate threshold.
  3. An action applies to one mailbox provider unless it says “the send”.
  4. Slowdowns end by themselves after their time. Pauses wait for someone who can resume: Linh Nguyen or Marcus Chen.
  5. Every action shows in the live monitor and in Events & sync.
