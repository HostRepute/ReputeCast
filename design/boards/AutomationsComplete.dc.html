1 · Manage flows A flow can be duplicated or deleted from the list, and a draft discarded in the editor. Deleting a live flow says how many contacts are in it and offers Pause instead.
2 · Who is at a step Each step opens the contacts at it, newest first, a page at a time. Removing someone from a flow is logged with your name. The contact's profile lists the flows they're in and where.
3 · Test an email and resume a paused flow An email step can be previewed as a real contact and sent as a test, as campaigns are. Resuming asks what to do with delays that ended during the pause, within warm-up caps and quiet hours.
4 · Triggers Up to three triggers start a flow; any of them enrols. Re-entry can wait N days, a date can fire at the contact's own time, a list flow can let contacts go when they leave the list, and two new triggers listen to segments and links.
5 · A marketing cap for the whole workspace At most N marketing emails in X days per contact, campaigns and automations together. Automation emails over the cap wait or are skipped, per step; Review & send shows how many the cap leaves out.
6 · Publishing a new trigger, and how a sequence performs A live flow's trigger changes by publishing; the diff says who is affected. Each email step shows delivered, human opens, clicks, unsubscribes and how many go on to the next email.
7 · Paid members With Stripe connected in live mode, six triggers start flows from paid subscriptions: becoming a paid member, changing tier, stopping paying, a trial about to end, and pausing and resuming payments. Becoming a paid member and stopping paying happen once per subscription; people who paid before this release never start them.
