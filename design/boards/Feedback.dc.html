Rules
Success and progress toasts use role="status"; errors use role="alert" and stay until dismissed. Undo is offered for deletes, moves and unsubscribes for 10 seconds; after that the audit log is the way back. Never a toast for something the page already shows (a badge changing, a count updating). Focus never jumps to a toast; it is readable at 4.5:1 on ink and sits above the phone tab bar.
