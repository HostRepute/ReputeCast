Design system · 1 of 5

Foundations: colour, type, spacing, radius, elevation, motion

Every value on this page is a token in the ReputeCast design system. The tokens are the source; the screens on the other pages are built from them. The internal name of this visual language is Clearwater: calm surfaces, honest numbers, one accent.

Next: components →

Colour

Text wears ink, never a status or chart colour. Teal is the single accent. Status colours come in a fill and text pair plus a solid bar colour, and always travel with a word or an icon. Chart series have their own fixed palette, validated for colour-vision deficiency.

Type

IBM Plex Sans for everything, IBM Plex Mono for code, ids, DNS records and terminal lines. Sentence case everywhere; the overline style is the only uppercase. Numbers are always tabular.

Spacing

A 4px grid. Cards are padded 18px 20px, inset panels 12px 14px. Content padding is 28px on desktop, 16px on tablets, 12px on phones; gaps between cards are 20, 16 and 12 at the same widths.

Motion

Motion belongs on the landing page and in live screens; the rest of the app stays still except for hover fills and skeletons. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion and renders its final state instead.
